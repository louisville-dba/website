## About the Downtown Business Association

The Louisville Downtown Business Association is a nonprofit dedicated to promoting and supporting the businesses, events, and historic character of our downtown district.

Our 90+ member businesses span Main Street and the surrounding blocks — independent restaurants, boutique retail, professional services, and the makers and artisans who give downtown its character.

## What we do

- Promote downtown as a destination for shopping, dining, and events
- Organize our signature **Street Faire** each summer
- Support the weekly **Farmers Market** from May through October
- Coordinate seasonal decorations, marketing, and beautification
- Advocate for downtown business interests with the City
